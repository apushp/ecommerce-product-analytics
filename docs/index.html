Do ads work, and for whom?

Criteo's experiment: ads lifted visits about 17% once treatment and control were made comparable, not the 27% a plain comparison shows. The effect is concentrated: with budget for 10% of users, uplift targeting captures 76% of the extra visits against 66% for targeting likely visitors.
